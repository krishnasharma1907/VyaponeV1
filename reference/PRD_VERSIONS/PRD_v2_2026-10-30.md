## 1. Executive Summary

VyapOne lets a reseller run a small digital-services business from a phone. They sign up a local business, Vya proposes and builds its site and tools in minutes, and the business then runs its day on VyapOne: it takes orders and bookings on WhatsApp, collects payment by UPI, sends GST-ready bills, and sends offers to its customer list. The reseller bills each client through VyapOne every month, and VyapOne earns from the reseller's plan, per-site add-ons, metered usage and a small cut of client billing.

**Why this shape:**

1. **The site alone doesn't sell.** Hostinger sells unlimited AI-built sites in India from ₹199/mo (source: Hostinger India pricing page, read 30 Oct 2026). A ₹399 plan for 3 sites can't win on price. It has to win on what the business can *do*.
2. **Indian small businesses run on WhatsApp and UPI,** not on websites and card gateways. The product meets them there.
3. **Resellers are the distribution.** Small businesses rarely buy software on their own; a local person they trust sets it up and keeps it running. GoHighLevel shows that agencies pay for a toolkit that lets them run and bill clients ($97–$497/mo; gohighlevel.com/pricing, read 30 Oct 2026). VyapOne is that idea, priced in rupees and built for a phone.
4. **Client billing makes VyapOne hard to leave.** When the reseller's own income is collected through VyapOne, switching to a cheaper builder means re-billing every client.

**Product promise:** *Build it in minutes. Run it every day. Get paid every month.*

## 3. Goals, Non-Goals and North Star

### 3.1 North-star metric

**Weekly Operating Sites (WOS):** live sites where the business recorded at least one real action in the week: an order, booking, bill, payment or broadcast. A published site nobody uses is not success.

### 3.2 Product goals

1. A reseller creates a client business and publishes a working site from a phone in under 15 minutes (target to validate).
2. Every live business can take an order or booking, collect UPI payment and send a bill on day one, with no gateway KYC.
3. The owner uses VyapOne daily through Vya's WhatsApp summary, even without opening the app.
4. The reseller's monthly client fee is collected automatically inside VyapOne.
5. Everything works in Hindi and English at launch, and by voice.

### 3.3 Business goals (pilot, hypotheses)

1. Prove resellers stay: monthly reseller churn clearly under 5% by pilot end.
2. Prove resellers earn: the median active reseller bills clients more than their VyapOne plan costs.
3. Prove per-site revenue: at least a third of live sites use a paid add-on or client billing.
4. Keep support load sustainable: measured support minutes per site trending down month on month.

### 3.4 Non-goals for MVP

- Full accounting, payroll, GST return filing or e-invoicing (IRN) integration.
- Logistics, delivery-partner dispatch, marketplace aggregation.
- Arbitrary code generation or custom-coded sites.
- Native iOS/Android apps (the PWA comes first).
- More than one fully operational vertical.
- Markets outside India.

## 4. Users, Roles and Hierarchy

### 4.1 Tenant hierarchy (kept from v1, extended)

`Platform → Reseller workspace → Business → Location`

- A **reseller workspace** owns many businesses and has team members.
- A **business** is one client brand. It has an owner, staff, one or more locations, one live site and its modules.
- A **location** is a physical outlet (timings, address, staff, stock, QR codes, Google profile). Multi-location is supported in the data model from day 1; the UI for it ships in Phase 2.
- A **DIY workspace** is a reseller workspace of one, owned by the business owner directly.

### 4.2 Roles

| Role | Who | Can |
|---|---|---|
| Platform admin | VyapOne team | Manage plans, templates, modules, moderation, support, payouts, audit |
| Reseller owner | Freelancer, student, agency founder | Create and manage businesses, set client fees, see earnings, white-label, manage team |
| Reseller team member | Agency staff | Work on assigned businesses only; no billing access unless granted |
| Business owner | Shop, salon, clinic owner | See and run their own business; pay the reseller; export their data |
| Business staff | Cashier, stylist, waiter, delivery person | Role-limited actions: take orders, make bills, manage bookings; no settings or exports |
| Customer | End buyer | Browse, order, book, pay, give feedback, opt in or out of messages |

### 4.3 Ownership and handover

- The **business owns its data**: customers, orders, bills and content. The reseller manages it on the owner's behalf.
- An owner can export their data at any time, and can move to another reseller or to a DIY plan through a controlled handover. This protects owners and builds trust in the reseller channel.
- A reseller cannot delete a business's customer data without the owner's confirmation.
- If a reseller stops paying, live sites get a grace period (open decision: length), then the owner is offered a DIY plan before anything goes offline.

### 4.4 Primary personas

| Persona | Snapshot | Wins when |
|---|---|---|
| **Rohit, student reseller** | 21, tier-2 city, good with phones, wants side income | He signs 3 shops in his first month and sees "₹3,000 collected" in his earnings dashboard |
| **Priya, small agency** | Runs a 3-person social-media agency | She adds websites + WhatsApp ordering to every retainer, white-labelled as her brand |
| **Sharma ji, café owner** | Speaks Hindi, lives on WhatsApp, distrusts apps | Orders arrive on WhatsApp in a clean format, payment is in by UPI, and a summary shows up every night |
| **Anjali, salon owner (DIY)** | Comfortable with Instagram | Clients book themselves, get reminders and stop no-showing |

## 5. Product Principles

1. **Phone first, always.** Every essential task is complete at a 390-pixel viewport, one-handed, on a slow connection.
2. **WhatsApp is a channel, not an integration.** Where the customer and owner already are, VyapOne shows up there.
3. **Zero setup before value.** UPI QR, click-to-chat and bills work without KYC, approvals or a gateway.
4. **Structured, not freeform.** Sites and tools are assembled from approved components and modules, never from generated code.
5. **AI proposes, people confirm.** Vya never publishes, sends, charges or deletes without confirmation.
6. **Truthful numbers.** A click is not an order; a review link opened is not a review. Metrics say exactly what happened.
7. **Global-ready, India-first.** No hardcoded currency, language, tax or phone format, even though India is the only launch market.
8. **Every feature earns its place.** If it doesn't drive a Weekly Operating Site, reseller earnings or retention, it waits.

## 6. User Flows

### 6.1 Reseller onboarding (target: first business started within 5 minutes)

1. Sign up with a phone number + OTP. Choose a language (English, Hindi at launch).
2. Short "how you earn" screen: what to sell, an illustrative client fee, how payouts work.
3. Optional 10-minute Academy quick-start (Phase 2 adds certification).
4. Free tier active: 1 live site with VyapOne branding. A 14-day Starter trial unlocks on adding a second business (hypothesis).
5. Land on **Create business**.

### 6.2 Create a client business (target: published in under 15 minutes)

1. **Pick a niche** from the catalogue (search + icons), or "Other business".
2. **Tell Vya about it** by voice, text or photos: a shop board, a menu card, a rate list, an Instagram profile link, a Google Maps link.
3. Vya extracts name, address, timings, items and prices into a **draft**, marks every guessed field as "to confirm", and asks only the niche questions that are missing.
4. **Vya proposal:** pages, modules, design direction, the WhatsApp number, the UPI ID and the plan impact ("uses 1 of your 3 sites"). The reseller confirms or edits.
5. **Generate** from approved components. A versioned draft opens in the mobile editor.
6. **Edit** by tapping sections, or by asking Vya ("make the menu photos bigger", "add Diwali offer banner"). Every change shows a preview and can be undone.
7. **Publish** to `business.vyapone.app` (subdomain naming is an open decision) or a custom domain.
8. **Go-live pack** generated automatically: main QR poster, table/counter QR, WhatsApp click-to-chat link, Instagram bio link, Google Business Profile update (Phase 2), "We're online" WhatsApp status image.
9. **Set the client fee** (e.g. monthly amount + optional setup fee) and send the owner a WhatsApp invite with a payment link.
10. The owner joins, sets a PIN, pays (in-app or marked "collected offline") and sees their dashboard.

### 6.3 A business owner's day (the retention loop)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| Morning | Vya's WhatsApp summary: "Yesterday: 14 orders, ₹6,240 in UPI, 2 unpaid bills. Send today's offer to 120 customers?" Owner replies "Yes" or edits it |
| During the day | New order → WhatsApp alert to the owner + confirmation to the customer. Owner taps Accept → status updates go out automatically |
| At the counter | Staff create a bill in 3 taps → the customer gets it on WhatsApp with a UPI link; the bill joins the customer's history |
| Evening | Repeat-order reminders go to customers due for a reorder (opt-in only) |
| Night | Day close: orders, bills, collected vs. pending, top items |

### 6.4 End-customer flow

Scan a QR or open the link (Google, Instagram, WhatsApp) → browse the menu/catalogue/services in their language → add to cart or pick a slot → enter a name and WhatsApp number → pay by UPI (or pay at counter) → confirmation on WhatsApp → status updates → optional feedback request → future offers only if they opted in.

### 6.5 Reseller's month

The earnings dashboard shows clients due, collected, failed and overdue; payouts on schedule; sites at risk (no activity in 7 days); upgrade prompts ("2 clients would benefit from Bookings"); new leads from the marketplace (Phase 3).

### 6.6 DIY owner flow (Phase 2)

Same as 6.2 but the owner is the builder. A "Get expert help" button routes them to a certified local reseller through the lead marketplace, so DIY feeds the channel instead of competing with it.

## 7. Vya — AI Assistant

### 7.1 Identity (kept)

**Name:** Vya · **UI label:** Vya · VyapOne AI · **Primary action:** Ask Vya

> Hi, I'm Vya. Tell me about your client's business, and I'll help you build it, run it and grow it.

### 7.2 Three jobs

| Job | For | What Vya does | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Builder** | Reseller, DIY owner | Onboarding questions, extraction from photos/voice/links, proposal, copy, translations, design choices, missing-info check before publish | MVP |
| **Daily assistant** | Business owner | Morning summary, nudges ("3 bills unpaid", "Ramesh usually orders on Fridays"), offer drafts, review-reply drafts, answers "how much did I sell this week?" | Summary + nudges in MVP; conversational Q&A Phase 2 |
| **Reseller coach** | Reseller | Pitch scripts per niche, proposal PDF for a prospect, upsell suggestions, at-risk client alerts | Phase 2 |

### 7.3 Where Vya lives

- In-app panel (all roles, permission-scoped).
- **On WhatsApp** for owners: summaries and one-tap replies ("Yes, send", "Edit", "Not today") through approved message templates and the 24-hour service window. This is how an owner who never opens the app still uses VyapOne daily.
- By voice: speak in Hindi or English; Vya replies in text, and in voice later.

### 7.4 Action model (kept from v1)

`Request → intent + permission check → proposed tool action → preview/diff → confirmation when material → validated execution → logged result`

Low-risk reversible actions (drafting copy, rearranging sections) apply to the draft with undo. Material actions need explicit confirmation: publishing, sending any broadcast, changing prices, charging a client, deleting anything, changing payment or WhatsApp settings.

### 7.5 Safeguards (kept and extended)

- Never invent prices, stock, timings, qualifications, legal approvals, property status, reviews or payment success.
- Every AI-extracted fact stays "to confirm" until a person confirms it.
- Broadcast copy shows the exact message, the recipient count and the estimated cost before sending.
- No review gating, fake reviews or incentives for positive reviews.
- Strict tenant scoping: Vya sees only the business and role in the current session.
- Per-plan AI credits, rate limits and abuse controls; a manual path for every AI action when AI is down.
- No exposure of system prompts, keys or other tenants' data.

### 8.1 Platform core (kept)

Authentication and recovery; workspaces, roles and permissions; businesses and locations; subscriptions, entitlements and usage metering; media library; notifications; audit logs; support inbox; module registry.

### 8.2 Website engine (kept)

- Structured pages and sections from approved responsive components; theme tokens.
- Navigation, footer, SEO metadata, sitemap, structured data (LocalBusiness schema) for local search.
- Preview, publish, versioning, rollback.
- Custom domain connection with automatic SSL.
- Multi-language pages: the same structure with translated content per locale.
- Lightweight public pages: fast on a low-end Android phone on 3G/4G; no heavy effects on public sites.

### 8.3 Catalogue and services (kept)

Categories; products or services; photos; price, MRP and tax rate; variants and add-ons; availability; search and filters; featured items. **New:** bulk import from a photo of a menu/rate card (Vya extraction, every row "to confirm"), a spreadsheet, or a WhatsApp Business catalogue (Phase 2).

### 8.4 WhatsApp layer (new, MVP)

WhatsApp-native everything, built in two levels so value doesn't wait on Meta approvals.

**Level 1: free, zero setup (MVP)**
- Click-to-chat buttons on every page (`wa.me` links with prefilled text).
- **Order-to-WhatsApp:** a cart checkout can send a formatted order message to the business's WhatsApp, and the order is also saved in VyapOne.
- Share intents: the owner shares a bill, booking confirmation, offer or catalogue item to WhatsApp in one tap.
- WhatsApp status creatives: auto-generated status images for offers and new items (Phase 2 when the social module lands).

**Level 2: WhatsApp Business Platform (metered add-on)**
- Business numbers onboarded through Meta's embedded signup, with VyapOne as a technology partner via a provider (provider choice is an open decision).
- Automatic **transactional messages**: order received/accepted/ready, booking confirmed, reminder before an appointment, bill with a UPI link, payment received.
- **Owner alerts** and the Vya daily summary.
- **Marketing broadcasts** to opted-in customers (Pro).
- **Catalogue sync** to the WhatsApp Business catalogue (Phase 2).
- Inbound replies land in a simple shared inbox per business (Phase 2).

**Rules**
- Templates per niche and language, pre-approved where possible; owners don't write raw templates.
- Opt-in is recorded per customer and channel, with a timestamp and source. Every marketing message has an opt-out.
- Meta's per-message charges vary by country and message category. VyapOne keeps a **cost table per country and category**, updated from Meta's published rates, and prices metered messages at cost plus margin (section 13). Exact India rates must be verified before launch pricing (not in the research notes).
- If Level 2 is not set up or fails, VyapOne falls back to Level 1 (share link) and shows the owner what wasn't sent.

### 8.5 Payments — UPI first (new, MVP)

**Stage 1: UPI QR / intent, zero setup (MVP)**
- The owner enters their UPI ID once (or scans their existing shop QR to read it).
- Every order and bill shows a **dynamic UPI QR** and an **"Pay by UPI" intent button** with the exact amount and a reference (bill or order number) prefilled.
- Money goes straight to the business's bank. VyapOne never touches it and takes no fee on these payments.
- **Honest limit:** with a plain UPI ID there is no automatic payment confirmation. The order or bill shows "Payment claimed by customer" until the owner or staff taps "Received" (one tap, from the WhatsApp alert or the app). The UI never shows "Paid" on a customer's claim alone.
- Reconciliation aid (Phase 2): match claimed payments against a bank/UPI statement export.

**Stage 2: payment gateway (Phase 2)**
- Gateway onboarding with KYC done by the provider (Razorpay remains a candidate; final choice is an open decision).
- Verified payment status from signed webhooks; refunds; payment links; UPI AutoPay for subscriptions where the provider supports it.
- Order status and payment status stay separate. Payment success comes only from server-side verification (kept from v1).

**Stage 3 (Phase 3+):** pay-later at counter tracking ("udhaar" ledger per customer), partial payments, deposits for bookings.

### 8.6 Bills and GST-ready invoices (new, MVP)

- Create a bill in 3 taps from an order, a booking, or from scratch (counter billing).
- Business details: name, address, GSTIN if registered, state for place of supply.
- Line items with HSN/SAC code, quantity, rate, discount and tax rate; CGST + SGST or IGST split computed by the system; round-off; bill of supply for businesses not registered under GST.
- Sequential, gap-free numbering per business and financial year; prefixes per location.
- Share as a WhatsApp message with a PDF and UPI link; print on a 58/80 mm Bluetooth thermal printer from the PWA where the device supports it (Phase 2).
- Credit notes for returns and cancellations; nothing is ever silently deleted.
- Exports for the accountant (CSV/Excel, monthly summary by tax rate).
- **Out of scope:** GST return filing and e-invoicing (IRN) integration. The tax rules and thresholds must be confirmed with a chartered accountant before launch; VyapOne gives the owner the data, not tax advice.

### 8.7 Customer list (CRM-lite) and broadcasts (new, MVP)

- A customer record is created automatically from every order, booking, bill or enquiry: name, WhatsApp number, visits, total spend, last order, favourite items, tags and notes.
- Consent per channel (transactional vs. marketing), recorded and auditable.
- Segments: "new this month", "not seen in 30 days", "top 20 customers", "ordered X", custom tags.
- **Broadcasts** (Pro): pick a segment and a template (offer, new item, festival greeting), Vya drafts it, the owner sees the exact text, recipient count and estimated cost, then confirms.
- **Repeat-order reminders** (Phase 2): based on each customer's own ordering pattern ("usually reorders every 7 days") or a fixed rule per item; opt-in only; capped per customer per week.
- Import contacts from the phone or a spreadsheet; imported contacts receive nothing marketing-related until they opt in.

### 8.8 Orders (kept, MVP for food/retail vertical)

Cart; server-validated prices; dine-in table, pickup or delivery context; order placement; WhatsApp confirmation; accept/reject with reason; status (accepted → preparing → ready → completed); payment status kept separate; cancellation; kitchen/counter view for staff; a sound + WhatsApp alert for new orders. Delivery fee and area rules are simple flat rules in MVP.

### 8.9 Appointments (kept, MVP if salon is the first vertical)

Services with durations and prices; staff and their schedules; blocked times; availability; double-booking prevention; booking requests or instant confirmation; WhatsApp confirmation and reminder; reschedule/cancel links; no-show marking; deposit by UPI (Phase 3).

### 8.10 Enquiries and leads (kept)

Configurable forms per niche; consent capture; source attribution (QR, Google, Instagram, WhatsApp); status pipeline; assignment; follow-up dates with WhatsApp reminders to staff; spam controls.

### 8.11 QR Studio (kept)

Permanent dynamic QR codes (main, table, counter, product, offer, booking, review, campaign) whose destination can change without reprinting; printable posters and table tents in the brand style; scan analytics per placement; scan-safe contrast and quiet zones.

### 8.12 Google Business Profile sync (new, Phase 2)

- Connect the owner's Google account (owner consent; API access needs Google's approval, which is a Phase 0 task).
- Push timings, special/holiday hours, website link, photos and offers ("posts") from VyapOne.
- Pull reviews into the reviews module; draft replies with Vya; the owner approves.
- Health check: missing hours, no photos, unanswered reviews.
- Fallback before API access: a guided checklist with deep links (the same tips as the @vyapone "get found online" carousel).

### 8.13 Reviews and feedback (kept rules, new surfaces; Phase 2)

- A private feedback form plus a direct Google review link for **all** customers, whatever their rating. **No review gating**, no incentives for positive reviews, no fabricated or auto-posted reviews (kept from v1).
- A review-request WhatsApp message after a completed order or visit (opt-in, capped).
- Vya drafts honest replies; the owner approves before posting.

### 8.14 Offers and loyalty (kept; offers Phase 2, loyalty Phase 3)

- Offers with validity windows, coupon codes and visible terms; an offer banner on the site; a QR per offer.
- Loyalty: visit- or spend-based stamps ("10th chai free"), tracked by phone number on bills, no separate app; balance shown on the WhatsApp bill. Abuse controls and an audit trail for adjustments.

### 8.15 Auto social posts and creatives (new, Phase 2)

- Turn a catalogue item, offer or festival into ready creatives: Instagram post (1:1, 4:5), story/WhatsApp status (9:16), with captions in the business's language.
- Built from brand templates (logo, colours, fonts) so output looks consistent, not random.
- Schedule or share now; direct Instagram publishing through Meta's APIs in Phase 3 (needs a business account and approval).
- Uses AI credits; every image and caption is a draft until approved.

### 8.16 Staff app and roles (new, MVP basic)

- The PWA has a staff mode: PIN login on a shared counter phone, role-limited home (orders, bills, bookings).
- Roles: manager, cashier, service staff, delivery. Permissions per role; every action logged with the staff member's name.
- Attendance and commissions are out of scope until Phase 3.

## 9. Reseller Toolkit (the moat)

### 9.1 Client billing inside VyapOne (new, MVP)

The single most important retention feature. The reseller's income flows through VyapOne.

- Per client, the reseller sets: a setup fee (one-time), a monthly or annual fee, a due date and what's included.
- The owner receives a WhatsApp invoice with a payment link; recurring collection via UPI AutoPay or card mandate where the provider supports it (Phase 2), payment link reminders before that.
- Collection runs through a payment provider's **split-settlement / marketplace product** (e.g. Razorpay Route, to be confirmed), so funds settle directly to the reseller's account minus VyapOne's platform fee. VyapOne does not pool or hold client money (legal review required before launch).
- **Platform fee:** a small percentage of the amount collected (hypothesis in section 13), plus the provider's own fees.
- The reseller gets automatic reminders, overdue tracking, receipts and GST invoices for their own services (if they're registered).
- **"Collected offline" mode** for clients who pay cash: free, tracked for the dashboard, no platform fee. This keeps VyapOne useful even when clients won't pay in-app, but in-app collection is the default the UI nudges towards.
- If a client stops paying the reseller, the reseller can pause premium modules for that client after a notice period. The site stays up for the grace period (section 4.3).

### 9.2 Reseller earnings dashboard (MVP basic, Phase 2 full)

Monthly recurring revenue from clients, collected vs. due vs. overdue, upcoming renewals, payouts, VyapOne costs, **net earnings**, per-client profitability, and at-risk clients (no activity, payment failed, owner never logged in).

### 9.3 White-label (Phase 2)

|---|---|---|
| Brand lite | Growth | Reseller logo and name in the owner's dashboard and on emails; "Powered by VyapOne" stays in the footer |

| Branded app | Scale (Phase 4) | A branded PWA/app icon on the owner's phone |

### 9.4 Team (MVP basic)

Invite team members, assign businesses, and limit billing visibility. Activity log per member.

### 9.5 VyapOne Academy and certification (Phase 2)

- Short video and text lessons in Hindi and English: how to find clients, pitch scripts by niche, pricing your services, setting up each vertical, handling support.
- Certification after a practical test (publish a real site that passes a checklist). Certified resellers get a badge, priority in the lead marketplace and a directory profile.
- Partnerships with colleges and skilling programmes to recruit students as resellers (go-to-market lever from the market note).

### 9.6 Lead marketplace (Phase 3)

- Businesses that sign up DIY and tap "Get expert help", or come in through VyapOne's own marketing, become leads.
- Leads are routed to certified resellers by location, language, niche and rating. A lead fee or revenue share (hypothesis) applies only when the lead converts.
- Owners rate resellers after 30 days; poor ratings reduce routing.

### 9.7 Referral programme (Phase 2)

- Reseller-to-reseller: both get a credit (e.g. a free month, hypothesis) when the referred reseller pays for their first month.
- Business-to-business: an owner who refers another owner gets a credit through their reseller.
- Fraud controls: credit only on a real paid month; caps per referrer.

### 9.8 Proposal kit (Phase 2)

Vya creates a one-page proposal for a prospect (with a live preview link of their draft site, built in 5 minutes during the pitch). "Show, don't tell" is the reseller's best sales tool.

## 13. Pricing and Packaging

**Every price in this section is a hypothesis to validate in the pilot.** Prices are monthly, in INR, before GST.

### 13.1 Design principles

1. **Free to start, paid to grow.** A reseller can build and demo for free; money starts when a client goes live.
2. **Revenue grows with each reseller's book**, through extra sites, per-site add-ons, usage and client billing, not only through plan upgrades.
3. **Never undercut the channel.** The DIY owner price is always above what a reseller pays per site, so resellers can add their service fee and still compete.
4. **Pass-through costs are metered.** WhatsApp, SMS and heavy AI use are paid per use, so a heavy user never makes a plan unprofitable.

### 13.2 Reseller plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (2 months free) | Live sites | Per site | Key extras |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Free** | ₹0 | — | 1 | — | VyapOne badge, subdomain only, base modules with fair-use caps, small AI allowance |
| **Starter** | ₹399 | ₹3,990 | 3 | ₹133 | Custom domains, client billing, earnings dashboard, 1 team seat |
| **Growth** | ₹999 | ₹9,990 | 8 | ₹125 | Brand-lite white label, 3 team seats, larger AI allowance |
| **Agency** | ₹1,999 | ₹19,990 | 20 | ₹100 | Full white label, 10 team seats, priority support, API (Phase 3) |
| **Scale** | Quote | Quote | 20+ | — | Volume pricing, branded app, account manager |

- A 14-day Starter trial unlocks when a Free user creates a second business.
- Annual prepay = 10 × monthly (about 17% off). It improves cash flow and cuts churn, and resellers can match it with annual client contracts.

### 13.3 Per-site add-ons

| Add-on | Price (hypothesis) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Extra live site | ₹149 Starter / ₹119 Growth / ₹99 Agency per site/mo | Priced so that Starter + 5 extra sites (₹1,144) costs more than Growth (₹999), keeping the upgrade path clear |

| ONDC listing (Phase 4) | To be set | Depends on partner costs |

The reseller decides whether to pass add-ons on to the client. VyapOne shows suggested client prices in the Academy, never enforced.

### 13.4 Metered usage (prepaid wallet per business or per reseller)

| Usage | How it's priced (hypothesis) |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp messages | Meta's rate for the country and message category + VyapOne margin (e.g. 15–25%), from the per-country cost table. Shown before every broadcast |
| AI credits | Monthly allowance per plan; top-up packs. One credit table for generation, images, voice and summaries |
| SMS | Cost + margin; used only as a fallback |
| Custom domain purchase | Connect your own domain free; buy via VyapOne at cost + small margin (Phase 2) |
| Storage | Fair allowance per site; paid packs beyond it |

Wallet rules: low-balance alerts on WhatsApp, an optional auto top-up, hard caps the owner sets, and no message is ever sent on credit the wallet doesn't have. Transactional messages (order/booking confirmations) get priority over marketing when the balance is low.

| Flow | VyapOne fee (hypothesis) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Customer → business by UPI QR/intent | 0% | Goes straight to the business's account |
| Customer → business via gateway (Phase 2) | 0% initially | Provider fees passed through. Whether to add a small platform fee later is an open decision; staying at 0% builds trust |
| **Business → reseller (client billing)** | **2% of amount collected** | Plus provider fees. 0% for "collected offline". For reference, Instamojo's Starter charges 5% + ₹3 per transaction (pricing page, 30 Oct 2026) |
| Lead marketplace (Phase 3) | Fee or share on converted leads | Only on conversion |
| Template/module marketplace (Phase 3) | Revenue share with creators | Creators keep the majority on templates |

### 13.6 Direct business owner (DIY) plans (Phase 2)

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Includes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Solo | ₹299 | ₹2,990 | 1 site, base modules, custom domain |
| Solo Pro | ₹499 | ₹4,990 | Solo + Pro pack |

A reseller's cost for one site with Pro is about ₹332 on Starter (₹133 + ₹199), below Solo Pro's ₹499, so the reseller channel stays competitive once their service is included. Every DIY dashboard has "Get expert help", which routes to the lead marketplace.

### 13.7 Counting rules (kept from v1, extended)

- One live site = one live business (not one page). Locations under a business don't use extra slots; a second brand does.
- Drafts use a separate, capped allowance.
- A custom domain doesn't use another slot. Archiving doesn't delete data immediately.
- On downgrade, the reseller chooses which sites stay live. No site disappears without warning, a grace period and a fallback (DIY offer to the owner).
- Customer payments to a business always belong to that business and stay separate from VyapOne billing.

### 13.8 Unit economics notes

| Item | Figure | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Starter ARPU | ₹4,788/yr (₹399 × 12) | Plan price |
| Blended reseller ARPU | ₹599–₹789/mo depending on plan mix | Market note, scenarios (assumed mixes) |
| Illustrative Starter reseller with 5 clients | ₹399 + 2 extra sites (₹298) + 3 Pro packs (₹597) + 2% of ₹5,000 client billing (₹100) = **₹1,394/mo (₹16,728/yr)** | Illustration only; client fee of ₹1,000/mo is an assumption |
| That reseller's net earnings | ₹5,000 collected − ₹1,394 = ₹3,606/mo before provider fees and their own costs | Illustration |
| Churn effect | At 5%/mo churn, about 46% of the base is lost in a year; holding 6,000 resellers would need about 300 new paying resellers every month | Market note (generic SMB SaaS benchmark, not India) |
| CAC ceiling | At a 70% gross margin (target, hypothesis), a 6-month payback allows about ₹1,675 CAC per Starter reseller (₹399 × 0.7 × 6) | Arithmetic on hypotheses |

**What this tells us:**
1. ₹399 alone needs volume and cheap acquisition. Paid ads will rarely fit under a ~₹1,675 CAC ceiling, so reseller acquisition must run on referral, the Academy, college partnerships and community.
2. Per-site revenue (extra sites, Pro packs, client billing, metered margin) is what turns a ₹4,788/yr reseller into a ₹15,000+/yr one. Track **revenue per live site** as closely as revenue per reseller.
3. Churn is the biggest risk to the model. Client billing, the daily Vya loop and annual prepay are the retention levers, and they're in the MVP for that reason.
4. Support cost can erase a Starter account's margin (the Dukaan lesson). Self-serve help, Vya and reseller-first support (the reseller is the first line for their clients) must keep support minutes per site falling.

### 13.9 Revenue outlook (from the market note, assumptions)

Base case on v1 prices, subscriptions + 20% add-ons: about **₹15 lakh in year 1, ₹1.66 crore in year 3, ₹5.18 crore in year 5**, with ~6,000 paying resellers by year 5. Strong case: ~₹21.5 crore in year 5 with ~20,000 resellers. These are scenario assumptions, not forecasts. v2's per-site add-ons, client-billing fee and DIY plans are upside not included in those numbers.

## 14. Architecture and Scalability

### 14.1 Starting stack (kept from v1, still a decision)

| Layer | Direction |
|---|---|
| App | Next.js + TypeScript, installable PWA (service worker, IndexedDB offline queue) |
| UI | Tailwind + accessible primitives (shadcn or equivalent) on VyapOne design tokens |
| Editor | Puck (candidate), constrained to VyapOne components, mobile-first |
| Database | PostgreSQL with row-level security |
| Auth / storage | Supabase is a candidate (phone OTP, storage); final choice is an architecture decision |
| Jobs and events | Managed queue + transactional outbox; scheduled workers for reminders and summaries |
| AI | Provider-neutral AI gateway (Vercel AI SDK or equivalent) with structured outputs |
| Payments | Gateway with split settlement (Razorpay candidate) + UPI intent/QR generator |
| Messaging | WhatsApp Business Platform via a provider (open decision), SMS fallback, email |
| Validation | Shared Zod (or equivalent) schemas on client and server |
| Testing | Unit/integration + Playwright journeys on mobile viewports |
| Observability | OpenTelemetry traces, structured logs, error tracking, per-tenant cost metrics |

### 14.2 Multi-tenant model

- One shared platform; every row carries `workspace_id` and `business_id` (and `location_id` where relevant).
- Authorization from explicit membership + role, enforced on the server and again by database row-level policies, so a modified URL or API call can't cross tenants.
- Public sites render per tenant from published config; no per-business deployments.
- Noisy-neighbour protection: per-tenant rate limits on APIs, AI, messaging and media.
- Data residency: primary data in an India region (DPDP-aware); the design allows regional cells later for global markets.

### 14.3 Structured site config (kept)

Sites are versioned JSON documents validated by schema: metadata, theme tokens, pages, ordered sections, component types, validated props, module bindings, locales. This enables mobile editing, Vya tool actions, safe rendering, diffs and rollback. **Generation selects and configures approved components; it never writes code.**

### 14.4 Module / plugin system

Each module ships a **manifest**:

- id, version, niches it suits, dependencies
- data schemas and migrations
- settings schema (rendered as forms automatically)
- UI surfaces: public components, dashboard screens, staff-mode screens
- events emitted and consumed (e.g. `order.created`, `bill.issued`, `payment.confirmed`)
- message templates per locale
- permissions it needs
- entitlement key and pricing key (Base, Pro, add-on)
- Vya tools it exposes, with schemas and confirmation level

First-party modules use the same SDK that marketplace creators will use in Phase 3, so the plugin system is tested from day 1.

### 14.5 Event-driven notifications and messaging

`Domain event → outbox → event bus → rules (who, which channel, which template, consent, quiet hours) → cost check against wallet → channel adapter (WhatsApp / SMS / email / push / in-app) → delivery status webhooks → message log`

- **Per-country cost table:** channel × country × message category → unit cost, currency and effective date. Pricing, wallet debits and margin reports all read this one table, so a new country is a data change, not a code change.
- Idempotent sends with dedupe keys; retries with backoff; dead-letter queue with alerts.
- Fallback rules (WhatsApp fails → share link to owner, or SMS where configured and paid).
- Quiet hours and per-customer frequency caps for marketing.

### 14.6 Global-ready from day 1 (cheap now, expensive later)

- **i18n:** every string in message catalogues (ICU format); locale per user, per business and per customer; RTL-capable layout tokens for Arabic later.
- **Money:** amounts stored as integer minor units + ISO currency code; plan prices per currency in a price book.
- **Tax:** a tax engine interface; GST is the first implementation (VAT for the Gulf later).
- **Payments and messaging:** adapter interfaces (UPI/Razorpay first; others later).
- **Phone, address, date and number formats** from locale data, never hardcoded.
- **Timezones** per business.

### 14.7 Cost controls (AI and WhatsApp)

- AI gateway: per-tenant credit ledger, per-plan budgets, model routing (small models for extraction and summaries, larger only where quality needs it), response caching for repeated content, and hard stops with a friendly manual fallback.
- Generation is mostly deterministic: presets and components do the heavy lifting; AI fills copy and choices. That keeps cost per generated site low and predictable.
- WhatsApp: estimated cost shown before every broadcast; wallet hard caps; transactional before marketing; daily spend anomaly alerts.
- Dashboards: AI cost per site, messaging margin per country, infra cost per live site, support minutes per site.

### 14.8 Observability and reliability

- Tracing across web → API → jobs → providers; structured logs with tenant ids (no personal data in logs).
- SLOs (targets, hypotheses): public site availability 99.9%; order/booking event to WhatsApp send under 30 seconds at p95; publish under 60 seconds at p95.
- Synthetic checks for the critical journeys; status page; on-call alerts for payment webhooks, message failures and publish failures.
- Backups with tested restores; per-business export.

### 14.9 Performance and scale targets (hypotheses)

- Public sites served from a CDN/edge cache from published config; a tiny JS budget; images resized and in modern formats.
- Dashboard usable on a low-end Android on 4G; offline-tolerant for bills and counter orders.
- Architecture sized for tens of thousands of live sites without redesign (the year-5 base case is ~26,000 businesses, per the market note).

## 16. Security, Privacy and Trust

Kept from v1 in full, plus:

- **Server-side authority:** prices, totals, entitlements and payment states are computed and verified on the server. Payment success comes only from verified provider signals or explicit owner confirmation for UPI; never from redirect parameters.
- **Webhooks** signature-verified, replay-resistant and idempotent.
- **Secrets** in a managed secret store; provider tokens per business encrypted; clients never see secrets.
- **Personal data:** customer phone numbers are the business's data, processed by VyapOne on its behalf. Purpose-limited, exportable, deletable on request. Designed for India's DPDP Act (obligations to be confirmed by legal counsel).
- **Consent and spam:** marketing only to opted-in customers; one-tap opt-out honoured everywhere; frequency caps. Protecting each business's WhatsApp number quality is part of the product.
- **Money:** VyapOne never pools client or customer funds; settlement goes through licensed providers.
- **AI transparency:** AI-written content is labelled as a draft until approved; no fake reviews, stats or testimonials on generated sites.
- **Abuse:** moderation for prohibited businesses and content; phishing and impersonation checks on new sites and custom domains.
- **Access:** OTP + device sessions; optional PIN for staff mode; re-confirmation for payouts and data export.

## 20. MVP Acceptance Criteria

The MVP is ready for a controlled pilot when:

1. A reseller signs up, creates a business and publishes it from a phone at a 390-pixel viewport, in Hindi or English.
2. Vya builds a proposal only from supplied information; every extracted fact is marked "to confirm" until confirmed.
3. Generation uses only approved components and presets; the site is editable, versioned and can be rolled back.
4. Plan limits (live sites, seats, AI credits) are enforced on the server.
5. A customer can complete the vertical's full flow: browse → order → UPI QR/intent → WhatsApp confirmation (or share-link fallback).
6. A "claimed" UPI payment is never shown as "received" until the owner confirms it.
7. Staff can create a GST-ready bill or bill of supply in 3 taps, share it on WhatsApp with a UPI link, and it works offline and syncs.
8. Every order, booking and bill creates or updates a customer record with correct consent state.
9. A broadcast shows recipients, exact text and estimated cost before sending; it reaches only opted-in customers and debits the wallet correctly.
10. The owner receives a correct morning summary on WhatsApp.
11. The reseller sets a client fee, the owner pays in-app, settlement splits correctly, and the earnings dashboard matches the provider's records.
12. No user can read or change another workspace's or business's data via UI, API or Vya.
13. Payment and messaging webhooks are verified, idempotent and replay-safe.
14. Public sites stay fast and usable when AI, effects or a provider is down.
15. Terms, privacy, refund, cancellation, support and data-retention policies are live.

## 23. Risks and Mitigations

| # | Risk | Why it's real | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Crowded, cheap market** | Hostinger sells unlimited AI sites from ₹199/mo; Instamojo and Durable have free tiers | Don't compete on the site. Compete on run + bill: WhatsApp, UPI, bills, CRM, client billing. Free tier removes the price objection at the start |
| 2 | **Low willingness to pay** | 46.2% of surveyed MSMEs are fully offline (India SME Forum); Khatabook lost ₹116 cr on ₹103 cr revenue in FY24 (YourStory) | The reseller sells and collects, not VyapOne. Price per site low, value per day high. Validate with pre-orders in Phase 0 |
| 3 | **Churn** (reseller and their clients) | Generic SMB SaaS churn is 3–5%/mo; at 5%, ~46% of the base goes in a year | Client billing (switching cost), daily Vya loop, annual prepay, at-risk alerts, owner handover instead of site loss |
| 4 | **WhatsApp API costs and rules** | Per-message charges by country and category; template approvals; number-quality limits | Level 1 free fallback; metered wallet with margin; cost shown before send; per-country cost table; opt-in and frequency caps protect number quality |
| 5 | **Support load (the Dukaan lesson)** | Dukaan cut ~90% of support for an AI bot (CNN, Jul 2023); live stores later fell to 2,084 from a 3,860 peak (Store Leads, Oct 2026) | Resellers are first-line support for their clients; Vya handles how-to; humans for money, publishing and outages; track support minutes per site; never remove human escalation |
| 6 | **Reseller quality** | Bad resellers create unhappy owners and churn | Academy + certification, owner ratings, data portability and handover rights for owners |
| 7 | **Payments and compliance** | Holding funds, GST bill formats and data protection carry legal risk | Licensed split settlement (no pooling), CA-reviewed bill templates, DPDP review, no tax-filing claims |
| 8 | **Platform dependency** | Meta, Google and payment providers can change rules, prices or access | Adapter interfaces, fallbacks for every channel, no single feature that dies if one API changes |
| 9 | **Scope creep** | The feature list in this PRD is long; a small team can't build it all | Strict MVP; a feature moves up a phase only when pilot data asks for it |
| 10 | **AI errors** | Wrong prices or timings on a live site damage trust | "To confirm" states, no auto-publish, version history, AI never invents facts |
| 11 | **Channel conflict with DIY** | DIY could undercut resellers | DIY priced above reseller per-site cost; "Get expert help" routes DIY users to resellers |
| 12 | **Going global too early** | Splits a small team across languages, payments and support | Global only after the India PMF gate (section 19) |

## 25. Definition of Done (MVP)

**Reseller signs up on a phone → creates a client business by voice or text → Vya proposes the site and modules → reseller confirms → VyapOne generates the site from approved components → reseller publishes and prints the QR → reseller sets the client's monthly fee and the owner pays in-app → a customer scans, orders, pays by UPI and gets a WhatsApp confirmation → staff confirm payment and send a GST-ready bill → the customer joins the business's customer list → next morning, Vya sends the owner a summary and offers to send today's offer → the reseller sees the client fee in their earnings.**

The MVP succeeds only if this loop is reliable, secure, understandable to a non-technical owner, usable from a phone, and repeated every week by real businesses. Visual polish supports that outcome; it doesn't replace it.
